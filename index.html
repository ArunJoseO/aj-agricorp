Our estate

Rooted in the
heart of spice country

Nestled in the mist-kissed elevations of the Idukki district, our estates sit at the epicentre of India's premier spice-growing belt, renowned for both fine green cardamom and Malabar's legendary black pepper. Shade canopy, rich laterite soil, and year-round highland humidity create an ideal microclimate for spices with unusual aromatic intensity.

Every capsule and berry is hand-harvested at peak maturity, sun-cured using heritage techniques, and graded through a multi-point quality matrix, so what reaches you, retail or bulk, carries the full integrity of single-origin provenance.

Spice knowledge

Sourcing questions, answered

A practical starting point for buyers comparing cardamom grades, Malabar black pepper, processing methods, and bulk procurement from Kerala.

What makes Idukki cardamom distinctive?

Our estates are in Idukki, Kerala, within a premier spice-growing belt known for green cardamom and Malabar black pepper. AJ AgriCorp Ventures supplies estate-direct cardamom lots graded by capsule size.

What are common cardamom grades?

Our current range includes 8 mm+ Emperor Green, 7.5 mm Premium Green, and 7 mm Standard Green cardamom. The range is offered for retail and bulk sourcing requirements.

What is Tellicherry black pepper?

Our Bite & Bloom range includes TGSEB, TGEB, MG-1, and MG-2 options. Flagship lots are garbled to Tellicherry Special Extra Bold specification, with TGSEB listed at 6.5 mm+.

Can you supply spices in bulk?

Yes. Bulk supply starts at 50 kg, with multi-tonne contract lots available subject to seasonal harvest and commercial terms. Buyers can request a 50 g graded sample before placing an order.

What specifications can buyers request?

Buyers can discuss grade, moisture, curing method, packaging, delivery requirements, and certification needs. Current stated moisture ranges are 8–12% for cardamom and 10–14% for black pepper, with custom requirements discussed during sourcing.

How do I request a bulk quote?

Use the bulk RFQ form and include the commodity, volume, preferred grade, and delivery requirements. The sourcing team aims to respond within one business day.
