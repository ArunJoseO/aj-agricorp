Our Estate

Rooted in the
Heart of Spice Country

Nestled in the mist-kissed elevations of the Idukki district, our estates sit at the epicenter of India's premier spice-growing belt — renowned for both the world's finest cardamom and Malabar's legendary black pepper. The combination of shade canopy, rich laterite soil, and year-round highland humidity creates an ideal microclimate for producing spices with unparalleled aromatic intensity.

Every capsule and berry is hand-harvested at peak maturity, sun-cured using heritage techniques, and graded through a rigorous multi-point quality matrix — ensuring that what reaches you, whether retail or bulk, carries the full integrity of single-origin provenance.

B2B & Wholesale

Built for
Enterprise Scale

AJ AgriCorp Ventures operates a dedicated bulk sourcing vertical for institutional buyers, FMCG companies, spice exporters, restaurant chains, and contract procurement desks. Our multi-commodity model — spanning premium cardamom and Malabar black pepper — is vertically integrated from cultivation to cured delivery, eliminating intermediaries and guaranteeing lot-level traceability at every step.

Step 01 — Inquiry
Submit RFQ with commodity, specifications, volume, and delivery timeline.
Step 02 — Sample Dispatch
Receive graded samples — cardamom or pepper — with COA and lab certification within 5–7 days.
Step 03 — Contract & Pricing
Flexible pricing: spot rate or long-term supply contract across commodities. Forward booking available.
Step 04 — Secured Dispatch
Tamper-evident packaging, cold-chain logistics, insurance-covered shipment for all commodity types.

Custom Quantities

From trial orders to multi-ton contract lots across cardamom and black pepper. MOQ: 50kg. Ceiling limited only by seasonal harvest yield.

Specialized Curing

Solar-cured, shade-dried, or kiln-processed to commodity spec. Custom moisture: cardamom (8–12%), black pepper (10–14%) on request.

Secure Logistics

Domestic & export-ready packaging. Air cargo or surface freight. Full e-way bill, GST invoicing, and insurance documentation.

Contract Farming

Multi-season forward contracts for volume buyers on cardamom and pepper. Guaranteed pricing bands with estate-backed supply assurance for 1–3 year terms.

Quality Assurance

Third-party lab testing for volatile oils, piperine content (pepper), aflatoxin, heavy metals, and moisture. COA provided with every bulk shipment.

White-Label Packaging

End-to-end packaging for domestic retail brands across spice categories. Custom pouches, branding application, and compliance-ready labeling available.
