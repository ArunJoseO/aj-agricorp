Our Estate

Rooted in the
Heart of Spice Country

Nestled in the mist-kissed elevations of the Idukki district, our 10-acre estate sits at the epicenter of India's premier cardamom growing belt. The combination of shade canopy, rich laterite soil, and year-round highland humidity creates the ideal microclimate for producing cardamom with an unparalleled aromatic intensity.

Every capsule is hand-harvested at peak maturity, sun-cured using heritage techniques, and graded through a rigorous quality matrix — ensuring that what reaches you, whether retail or bulk, carries the full integrity of single-origin provenance.

B2B & Wholesale

Built for
Enterprise Scale

AJ AgriCorp Ventures operates a dedicated bulk sourcing vertical for institutional buyers, FMCG companies, spice exporters, restaurant chains, and contract procurement desks. Our vertically integrated model — from cultivation to cured delivery — eliminates intermediaries and guarantees traceability at every step.

Step 01 — Inquiry
Submit RFQ with specifications, volume, and delivery timeline.
Step 02 — Sample Dispatch
Receive graded samples with COA and lab certification within 5–7 days.
Step 03 — Contract & Pricing
Flexible pricing: spot rate or long-term supply contract. Forward booking available.
Step 04 — Secured Dispatch
Tamper-evident packaging, cold-chain logistics, insurance-covered shipment.

Custom Quantities

From 50kg trial orders to multi-ton contract lots. Minimum order: 50kg. Maximum: limited only by seasonal harvest yield.

Specialized Curing

Solar-cured, shade-dried, or kiln-processed based on buyer specifications. Custom moisture content (8–12%) on request.

Secure Logistics

Domestic & export-ready packaging. Air cargo or surface freight. Full e-way bill, GST invoicing, and insurance documentation.

Contract Farming

Multi-season forward contracts for volume buyers. Guaranteed pricing bands with estate-backed supply assurance for 1–3 year terms.

Quality Assurance

Third-party lab testing for volatile oils, aflatoxin, heavy metals, and moisture. COA provided with every bulk shipment.

Export Ready

APEDA & SPICES BOARD registered. Phytosanitary certification available. CIF/FOB pricing for international buyers on enquiry.
